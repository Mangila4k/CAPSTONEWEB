About PLSNHS

PLSNHS, Integrated Development of an Educational Management System For Placido L. Señor National High School is a modern online enrollment system. We streamline the admission process, making it easier for students, parents, and administrators to manage enrollments efficiently.

  • Paperless enrollment process
  • Automated status notifications
  • Integrated document tracking
  • 24/7 accessibility
